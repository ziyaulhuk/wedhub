Privacy Policy — WedHub

Last updated: October 16, 2026

WedHub ("the App") is a wedding invitation app. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no data entry of any kind.

2. Analytics & Advertising

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services of any kind.

3. Features That Interact With Other Services

  • Call button: opens your phone's built-in dialer with a pre-filled number. Any call is placed through your device and carrier, which are governed by their own policies.
  • Directions button: opens Google Maps in your external browser. Google handles that interaction under its own privacy policy.

4. Internet Access

The App requests standard internet permission so its interface can function. No personal data is sent over the network by the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
