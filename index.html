The Story

2018 — The invite was an app

Instead of paper invitation cards, I built our wedding invitation as an Android app and used a Bluetooth beacon to push the Play Store install link straight to guests' phones — one tap away from installing.

The original app

The first version (Ionic 3 + Firebase) did more than look pretty: it took your RSVP, added the wedding to your phone calendar automatically, and reminded you a day before — and two hours before — the big day.

Today — rewritten from scratch

This is a ground-up rewrite in TypeScript, Vite and Capacitor. It asks nothing of you — no accounts, no data collected — and works fully offline. The original 2018 code lives on in this project's legacy folder.
